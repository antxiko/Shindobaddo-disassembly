Sindbad crosses seven islands of platforms and ladders, knocks his enemies down with his scimitar or with a somersault and, once he has felled the ones that open the island, looks for the way out; a giant bird carries him from island to island. The whole game runs in the interrupt. The title comes from running the ROM, checked against openMSX; the seven islands, from the recorded RAM; the game in GIF and the tunes from its own engine, identical to openMSX frame by frame.

Casio - (c) 1986 - 16 KB cartridge at 0x4000 - MSX - Dump d120ca30...

There are no screenshots of the game here. The title comes from running the ROM (checked against openMSX), the islands are built in RAM by the ROM's code and the GIFs are the ROM running in openMSX: on every frame the VRAM, the RAM and the VDP registers are logged, and our own code paints them.

What is inside

The game

  • The game: the intro, the title, the controls, the scimitar and the somersault, the power, the lives and the points.
  • The islands: the seven islands whole, how they are built, the enemies, the way out and the bird.
  • The sprites: Sindbad, the giant bird and the enemies, drawn by running the game's own routines.
  • The texts: every text in the ROM, with its translation.
  • The music: the five tunes, from its own sound engine and checked against openMSX.

Curiosities

  • Curiosities: keys at power-on: endless lives and the starting island, the game in the interrupt, jumps into the middle of instructions and code nobody calls.
  • Secrets and bugs: what was searched for: the keys at power-on, the controls, and the score past a million that drags the high score down to 400.

Under the hood

  • Under the hood: rebuilding the ROM, the BIOS it uses, the states, the RAM and the tools.
